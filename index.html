Elements

Symbols

On the “Dieline” tab work right on the sheet: click to select, drag to move (also onto another panel or the inside), corners — scale, sides — crop a logo or stretch a shape, round handle — rotate (Shift — 15° steps). While dragging, red guides show when it lines up with the panel centre, edges, safe zone or other elements, and red numbers show the gaps to the panel edges in mm (turn off with “Snap”). Keys: arrows move 1 mm (Shift — 10 mm), Shift+H / Shift+V flip, ⌘/Ctrl+D duplicate, Delete removes, Esc deselects.

Logo: a PNG with a transparent background, or an SVG. Inside you can print, say, “Thank you for your order!” on the lid.
